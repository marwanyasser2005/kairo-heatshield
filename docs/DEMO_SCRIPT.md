# KAIRO HeatShield — three-minute demo script

## 00:00–00:20 — The problem

“A city can receive a detailed temperature surface and still not know what to investigate first. KAIRO turns heat intelligence into an evidence-backed priority queue for urban resilience teams.”

Open `/presentation`. Point to the top proof strip: one verified FortyGuard activity, 1,055 normalized TCM tiles, and a clearly separate Phoenix Scenario.

## 00:20–00:50 — Prove the live integration

Open `/dashboard`, choose **Data source**, then **Open verified result**.

“This is a completed Phoenix FortyGuard TCM activity. KAIRO normalized 1,055 tiles from the returned `average_temperature` field. The narrow observed range is shown with a labeled relative scale. No canopy, anomaly, or action data is invented in Live Mode.”

## 00:50–01:25 — Move from heat to priority

Return to the Phoenix Scenario and open **Hotspots**.

“The scenario is deliberately labeled. KAIRO ranks zones using temperature, persistence, local deviation, surrounding intensity, and exposure context. Every priority keeps its evidence and source class.”

Select the first hotspot and call out its temperature, anomaly, persistence, canopy sample, and priority score.

## 01:25–01:55 — Show measurable decision outputs

Open **Cool route**.

“KAIRO compares the shortest path with the lowest-exposure alternative and reports exposure saved, peak-temperature difference, and added distance. This is a screening aid, not a navigation or safety directive.”

Open **Cooling lab** and move the canopy slider.

“The lab reports an estimated temperature and exposure reduction with an uncertainty band. The result is explicitly a screening estimate, not a forecast or guaranteed outcome.”

## 01:55–02:25 — Explain, do not overclaim

Open **Correlations**.

“KAIRO calculates Pearson and Spearman associations across 30 matched zones, exposes the sample size, and states that scenario association is not causation. The 2022 Phoenix canopy sample is disclosed separately from modeled context.”

## 02:25–02:50 — Close the loop

Open **Actions** and export the field brief.

“The output is not a construction order. It is an assessment brief that keeps the evidence, limitations, and next question together for a city team.”

Optionally open **Analyst** to show the bounded tool trace.

## 02:50–03:00 — Final line

“FortyGuard provides the temperature intelligence. KAIRO turns it into decisions while keeping the boundary between evidence and scenario visible.”
